From the stories we grew up with

Even the Gods Had Their Moments

Even they misread each other, said things in a moment of hurt, and still found their way back.

Vishnu & Lakshmi · The origin of Dhanteras

The Sugarcane Field

During a visit to Earth, Lord Vishnu asked Mata Lakshmi to stay behind while he travelled South, but she followed him anyway. Along the way, she picked a stalk of sugarcane from a field without the farmer's permission. Annoyed, Vishnu cursed her to serve as a humble maid in the farmer's household for 12 years. Her presence blessed the farmer with immense wealth, and when Vishnu returned for her, he humbly compensated the farmer before bringing Lakshmi back home.

However long it took, he came back for her.

Vishnu & Lakshmi · The tale of Hayagriva

The Misunderstood Smile

In a light moment, Lord Vishnu smiled, but Mata Lakshmi read it as mockery directed at her. Hurt, she cursed him that his head would fall off — and during a battle, his bowstring snapped and severed it. Yet this "mistake" served a hidden purpose: Vishnu's head was replaced with that of a horse, making him Lord Hayagriva, the only form that could defeat the demon Hayagreekasura.

One misread moment — and still, it led somewhere good.

Vishnu & Lakshmi · Ucchaihshravas

The Divine Horse

Watching the celestial horse Ucchaihshravas approach Vaikuntha, Lakshmi became so absorbed in its beauty that she didn't hear Lord Vishnu calling her. Annoyed, he cursed her to be born on Earth as a horse. Realising he had been too harsh, Vishnu took the form of a horse himself to join her, doing penance beside her so they could break the curse together.

He admitted he was too harsh — and came to stand beside her.

Shiva & Parvati · Lake Mansarovar

The Cosmic Lesson

Lord Shiva was explaining the secrets of the universe to Parvati, but she was distracted by the beauty of Lake Mansarovar. Annoyed, Shiva said that if she loved fish and water so much, she should live among fisherfolk — and she was born on Earth as a fisherman's daughter. Missing her deeply, Shiva disguised himself as a fisherman, cast a giant net to solve her village's trouble, won her hand again, and brought her home.

He missed her too much to stay apart.

If even they had their moments and always found their way back to each other, so can we.
